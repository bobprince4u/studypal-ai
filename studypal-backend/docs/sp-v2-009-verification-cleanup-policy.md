# SP-V2-009 verification cleanup policy

This policy applies only to the three dedicated local-development accounts established by SP-V2-009 and identified by the private verification credential manifest, successful authentication, immutable IDs 1, 2 and 4, and their original evidence. It is authorized by the request to establish and execute verification cleanup. No real user, account prefix sweep, production database or recovery database is a cleanup target.

Before deletion, identify the configured loopback development database, verify all account IDs/names against the manifest and authentication, inventory owned records, and take a verified full PostgreSQL custom backup plus copies/hashes of the two owned document files. Abort on unexpected verification account IDs or unexpected owned resource counts. Preserve evidence privately under the ignored data directory.

Delete the two material files through authenticated owner-scoped material deletion. Delete only the exact three matching users inside a transaction; use the existing foreign-key cascades for their learning data and sessions. Compare unrelated learning-data snapshots before/after and roll back the transaction if they change. Remove only hashed per-account/per-user limiter keys; preserve shared IP buckets and unrelated counters. Verify old cookies are rejected, owned records/files are absent, other records are unchanged, and final ownership integrity passes.

Remove the private raw credential manifest and obsolete local TLS private key after successful cleanup. Retain private backup archives, document recovery copies and safe evidence for review; these are recovery artifacts, not live accounts or public source files. Keep the isolated recovery database until the user explicitly requests its disposal. Never delete or restore over the real database, truncate tables, drop schemas, or remove unrelated data. Public deployment verification would use separately identified accounts and a separately authorized environment; none exists yet.
